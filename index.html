Quick rules used in this game
  • 8x8 board; pieces move on dark squares only.
  • Regular pieces move diagonally forward by one square.
  • Captures can chain in one turn, but are shown as suggestions for you.
  • Reach the far row to become a king (turn ends there).
  • Kings move and capture diagonally forward and backward.
  • You win if opponent has no legal moves.
  • Gold-ring squares are recommended targets for your selected piece; blue dots are legal targets for that piece.
  • Piece colors match the standard board (red vs black); use You play as to sit on either side.
